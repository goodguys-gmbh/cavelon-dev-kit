# Shipping

- We ship to Germany, Austria and Switzerland.
- An order placed before 14:00 CET on a business day leaves the warehouse the
  same day.
- Delivery takes 1 to 3 business days in Germany and 3 to 5 business days to
  Austria and Switzerland.
- Shipping is free from 50 EUR; below that it costs 4.90 EUR.
- Every order gets a tracking link by email when it leaves the warehouse.
