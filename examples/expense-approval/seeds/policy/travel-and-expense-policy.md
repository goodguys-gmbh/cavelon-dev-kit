# Travel and Expense Policy of Alpenwerk GmbH

Alpenwerk GmbH is a fictional company; this policy is an example.

## R1 Scope

- **R1.1** This policy applies to every employee of Alpenwerk GmbH who spends
  money for the company: travel, accommodation, meals, business meals and work
  equipment.

## R2 General principles

- **R2.1** Spend the company's money as if it were your own: choose the most
  economical option that serves the purpose.
- **R2.2** Every expense needs a receipt. An expense without a receipt is not
  reimbursed, except public transport tickets up to 10 EUR.
- **R2.3** Submit an expense within 30 days of the date it was incurred.
- **R2.4** An expense request states the name of the requester, the cost
  centre, the business purpose, the date, the gross amount with its currency
  and the category (travel, accommodation, meals, business meal, work
  equipment).

## R3 Travel

- **R3.1** Travel by train in second class. First class is allowed when the
  journey takes more than 4 hours.
- **R3.2** Fly only when the train takes more than 4 hours, and only in economy
  class. Business class is excluded.
- **R3.3** A private car is reimbursed at 0.30 EUR per kilometre.
- **R3.4** A taxi is reimbursed up to 50 EUR per ride. Above 50 EUR the request
  needs a reason, such as no public transport, a late hour or heavy luggage.

## R4 Accommodation

- **R4.1** A hotel night, breakfast included, costs at most: Munich 180 EUR,
  Vienna 160 EUR, Salzburg 130 EUR, any other place 120 EUR.
- **R4.2** A night may cost up to 20 % more than the maximum with a reason,
  such as a trade fair or a fully booked city; the head of department decides.

## R5 Meals while travelling

- **R5.1** A trip of more than 8 hours on one day earns a meal allowance of
  14 EUR; a full day away earns 28 EUR.
- **R5.2** A meal the company or a customer provides reduces the allowance by
  a third of the daily rate for each meal.

## R6 Business meals

- **R6.1** A business meal costs at most 70 EUR per person, drinks and tip
  included.
- **R6.2** The receipt of a business meal names the participants and the
  occasion.
- **R6.3** A meal with colleagues only is reimbursed only as a team event that
  the head of department approved beforehand.
- **R6.4** Alcohol is reimbursed only when external guests are entertained.

## R7 Work equipment

- **R7.1** Work equipment up to 250 EUR may be bought without prior consent.
- **R7.2** Work equipment from 250 EUR to 1,000 EUR needs the prior consent of
  the team lead.
- **R7.3** Work equipment above 1,000 EUR needs two comparison offers.
- **R7.4** IT devices are procured by the IT department only. An IT device
  bought privately is not reimbursed.

## R8 Approval

- **R8.1** A person approves every expense: up to 500 EUR the team lead, up to
  2,000 EUR the head of department, above 2,000 EUR the managing director.
- **R8.2** Nobody approves their own expense.
- **R8.3** A rejection names the rule the expense violates.
- **R8.4** An expense that violates a rule may still be approved as an
  exception, with a reason; the approval names the exception.

## R9 Payment

- **R9.1** An approved expense is paid with the next payroll.
